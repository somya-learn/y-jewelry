The Story Circle

A Stories page, with the border on its main picture.

NOWGround #F6F1E8 · picture background #E9E1D2 · no borders
PROPOSEDGround #F8F4EC · picture background #EFE9DE · warm white border (#FFFDF9) on Stories pictures only
Why the border is only on Stories. A border on every picture turns a product grid into a scrapbook and makes the jewellery smaller on a phone. On Stories, it marks the picture as a memory: things with a border are kept; things without one are to buy.
Why only one step lighter. Much lighter and Y starts to look like every white jewellery site, and the border disappears. The ground must stay a little darker than the border for it to show. Navy text still passes contrast on both.
Made to wear. Made to keep.
